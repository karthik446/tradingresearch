# Hints: w4l4.3

## Hint 1

The distance to the sma_50 is measured in ATRs and carries a sign: below the average it is negative, above it positive. A long trades with the trend when price is above the average, a short when it is below. So "against the trend" is a direction and a sign that disagree, and a distance of exactly zero disagrees with nobody. The rule was pre-registered for one horizon only; any other horizon passes before you look at anything else. A missing value is not a pass: nobody can show the thesis is with the trend.

## Hint 2

The registry is the only door the challenger uses, so the filter does nothing until it has an entry there, under the name the chapter and the shipped tests use. The change file must name that same entry. Its Prereg is not yours to tune: section 8.2 fixes the metric, the pass rule, the guard, the margin and the sample size, and the journal has the one place that writes them out. The registration time must fall after one session's open and before the next one's, with its time zone.

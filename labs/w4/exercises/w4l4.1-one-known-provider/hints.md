# Hints: w4l4.1

## Hint 1

A route has up to three parts: the client, the host that served the call, and sometimes a tier or quantization after it. Only the host says who served the call, so take it out first and judge only it. A provider's name can come back in any case, and so can the word for "nobody said": compare without case.

## Hint 2

Clean is the narrow answer: the openrouter client and a host that names one provider. Everything else is not clean, including the routes that look harmless: the client with nothing after it, the client and an empty host, and a host that lists two providers. Then the record check is one condition with two parts: a trainable record and a route that is not clean. A restricted record never needs to say who served it, so it is never refused for its route.

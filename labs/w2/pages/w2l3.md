### w2l3.1 a long's stop

**Concept.** A level is a price the market has turned at before. A long's stop belongs below the nearest level under the entry: if the stop sits between that level and the entry, an ordinary retest of the level can take it out while the idea is still alive. "Nearest" means the highest level under the entry, whatever order the levels are stored in. A stop exactly on the level fails. A level exactly at the entry is neither under nor over it. If no level sits under the entry, the stop half holds. [Level](concepts.md#level).

**You edit** `src/tradingresearch/checks.py`, function `p4_levels_coherent` ([stub](../exercises/w2l3.1-long-stop/stub/src/tradingresearch/checks.py)). The stub also adds the `Level` import and `TARGET_ALLOWANCE_ATR = 0.5` near the top of the file:

```python
def p4_levels_coherent(thesis: Thesis, levels: tuple[Level, ...], atr14: float, reference_close: float) -> bool:
    """True if the stop sits beyond the nearest level against the trade and the target at or before the next level
    with the trade (plus 0.5·ATR). A side with no level holds."""
    # TODO(w2l3.4) compare at cents: levels are stored unrounded (hvn 343.40978) while the model quotes cents (entry
    #   343.41). Write cents(price) above this function, the price rounded to 2 decimals, and use it on the entry, the
    #   stop, the target and every level price. The allowance stays exact.
    entry, stop, target = entry_price(thesis, reference_close), thesis.stop, thesis.target
    prices = [level.price for level in levels]
    if thesis.direction == "long":
        # TODO(w2l3.1) a long's stop: strictly below the nearest level under the entry. A level exactly at the
        #   entry is neither under nor over it; with no level under the entry this half holds.
        stop_ok = True
        # TODO(w2l3.2) a long's target: at or below the next level over the entry plus TARGET_ALLOWANCE_ATR * atr14
        #   (exact, inclusive); with no level over the entry this half holds.
        target_ok = True
    else:
        # TODO(w2l3.3) a short mirrors both halves: its stop beyond the nearest level over the entry, its target at
        #   or above the next level under the entry minus the allowance.
        stop_ok = target_ok = True
    return stop_ok and target_ok
```

### w2l3.2 a long's target

**Concept.** A long's target should not ask price to break through the next level over the entry. That level is resistance, and a target beyond it needs the breakout as well as the move. The spec allows a little slack: the target may sit up to half an ATR past the level (`TARGET_ALLOWANCE_ATR * atr14`), and reaching that limit exactly still passes. As with the stop, "next" means the lowest level over the entry, a level at the entry does not count, and with no level over the entry the target half holds. [Level](concepts.md#level), [ATR allowance](concepts.md#atr-allowance).

### w2l3.4 the cents amendment

**Concept.** Your P4 now follows the spec, and observe shows the chapter's six at 4/6. The chapter's table says 2/6. The gap is in the data, not the rule. analyze stores levels as computed floats (hvn 343.40978), while the thesis model sees them rounded and quotes cents (entry 343.41). On raw floats, a level less than a cent from the entry counts as a real level under or over it. AAPL swing passes only because hvn 343.40978 counts as support under 343.41. XOM's two theses pass because hvn 163.37702 counts as support under 163.38, which hides that their stop of 160.11 sits on low_20 160.1101. JPM swing fails because hvn 335.11379 counts as resistance over 335.11. The author amended the spec at `week-2-end`: round the entry, the stop, the target and every level price to cents before the rule. A level that rounds to the entry is at the entry and ignored, and a stop that rounds onto a level is on it and fails. The ATR is not a price, so the allowance stays exact. [Cents](concepts.md#cents).

**You edit** `checks.py`: a `cents(price)` helper above `p4_levels_coherent`, and the two lines that read the prices ([stub](../exercises/w2l3.4-cents/stub/src/tradingresearch/checks.py)):

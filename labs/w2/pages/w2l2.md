### w2l2.1 a long's invalidation

**Concept.** The invalidation is the price that says the idea was wrong. For a long it has to sit where price goes on the way to the stop: at or above the stop (equal is fine: the stop is the invalidation) and below the entry (equal to the entry would fire on the fill). Above the entry it is on the profit side and says nothing about the idea failing. [Invalidation](concepts.md#invalidation).

**You edit** `src/tradingresearch/checks.py`, function `p3_invalidation_testable` ([stub](../exercises/w2l2.1-long-invalidation/stub/src/tradingresearch/checks.py)):

    """True if the invalidation lies between entry and stop (stop included), so it can be reached before the stop."""
    # TODO(w2l2.1) a long: the invalidation lies between the stop (equal to the stop passes) and the entry (equal to

    # TODO(w2l2.2) a short mirrors the long rule. A market_open entry has no price yet (entry.price is None): its
    #   entry is the snapshot's last close, reference_close.

### w2l2.2 a short, and a market_open entry

**Concept.** A short loses when price rises, so the rule mirrors: the invalidation sits above the entry (equal fails) and at or below the stop (equal passes). A market_open entry fills at the next session's open, so `entry.price` is None when the thesis is written; P3 takes the snapshot's last close, `reference_close`, as its entry. `checks.py` already has `entry_price(thesis, reference_close)` for that, because P4 needs the same entry; using it here keeps the two checks on one definition. [Invalidation](concepts.md#invalidation), [market_open entry](concepts.md#market_open-entry).

**You edit** `p3_invalidation_testable` ([stub](../exercises/w2l2.2-short-and-market-open/stub/src/tradingresearch/checks.py)):

```python
    entry, invalidation, stop = thesis.entry.price, thesis.invalidation_price, thesis.stop
    if thesis.direction == "long":
        return stop <= invalidation < entry
    # TODO(w2l2.2) a short mirrors the long rule. A market_open entry has no price yet (entry.price is None): its
    #   entry is the snapshot's last close, reference_close.

```

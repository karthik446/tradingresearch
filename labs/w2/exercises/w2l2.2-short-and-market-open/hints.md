## Hint 1

A short loses money when price rises. Mirror the long's picture: which side of the entry is the stop on now, where
may the invalidation sit, and which end of that range is still allowed? MSFT day (entry 496.5, stop 500.6) is a
short to draw it on; 500.601 sits a tenth of a cent beyond its stop.

## Hint 2

What is the entry of a thesis that buys or sells at the next open? It has no price of its own, so the check is
handed one to stand in for it. Look for what in the file already knows which entries need that stand-in, and ask
whether a short at the open needs it just as much as a long does.
